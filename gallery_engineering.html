AIGC for Culture

My work focuses on Multimodal AI and AIGC (AI-Generated Content), using AI-generated video, visual storytelling, and digital media to support business planning, brand promotion, and cultural communication through my startup, CongZhi. Through this work, CongZhi explores a new direction — AIGC for Culture, applying generative AI to cultural communication, branding, and creative content production.
